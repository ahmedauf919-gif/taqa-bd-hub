The Challenge
Saudi Agriculture's Energy Burden
⛽
Diesel Dependency
Saudi farms spend 25–40% of operating costs on diesel — for generators, pumps, and heating. Prices volatile, supply unreliable in remote areas.

Climate Extremes
Saudi Arabia's temperatures range from -5°C to +50°C. Year-round controlled environments are essential but energy-intensive to maintain.

Water Scarcity
Groundwater depletion drives demand for energy-intensive desalination and pumping. More efficient fuel = fewer water m³ per energy unit.

Focus Area
Poultry & Livestock — Gas Makes the Difference
🐣
Chick Brooding (Day 1–14)
Gas brooders maintain 32–35°C in the chick zone. Consistent warmth is critical — temperature drops by even 3°C in the first week increases mortality by up to 15% and stunts growth. Gas brooders maintain temperature without the power cuts that disrupt electric systems.
🐓
Grow-Out Phase (Day 14–42)
Radiant tube heaters maintain 18–24°C across the full shed. Even heat distribution eliminates cold corners where birds pile up (causing injuries). Gas systems eliminate the noise of diesel generators that stress birds and reduce growth rate.
🏭
Slaughter & Processing
Scalding tanks, defeathering, chilling, and sterilisation all use heated water. Gas water heaters provide continuous supply at the volumes needed — electric cannot scale to meet processing demand peaks.
📊
FCR Improvement
Consistent temperatures on gas heating improve Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR) by 0.08–0.12 points (e.g., from 1.72 to 1.62). At SAR 0.85/kg feed and 50,000 birds per cycle, that's SAR 65,000+ saved per cycle on feed alone.

Water & Pumping
Gas-Powered Irrigation — More Water, Less Cost
⚙️
Gas Engine Pump Sets
Natural gas reciprocating engines (200–2,000 kW) drive centrifugal or turbine pumps directly. More efficient than diesel at the same output, with lower fuel cost per m³ pumped. Suitable for deep wells (200–500m depth).
💡
Gas CHP for Irrigation Districts
Combined Heat & Power (CHP) units generate electricity for pumps while capturing waste heat for farm buildings or greenhouses. Electrical efficiency 38–42% + heat recovery 40% = 80%+ total energy use efficiency.
📡
SCADA-Integrated Smart Pumping
TAQA Arabia's SCADA system monitors gas flow, pressure, and engine performance. Integration with your irrigation controller allows gas consumption to follow crop demand automatically.

Food Processing
Gas for Saudi Food Production & Processing
📅
Date Processing
Gas-fired drying tunnels for Medjool, Ajwa, Sukkari dates. Precise 60–80°C drying temperatures reduce moisture to 12–14% without caramelisation. Output: 50–200 tonnes/day on a single gas line.
🌾
Alfalfa Dehydration
High-temperature gas dryers (400–600°C combustion, 120°C product) for alfalfa pellet production. Gas replaces bunker fuel in rotary drum dryers — 45% lower fuel cost per tonne of dried product.
🥛
Dairy Pasteurisation
Gas-fired plate heat exchangers and steam generation for HTST and UHT pasteurisation. Consistent steam pressure critical for regulatory compliance (Saudi Food Authority standards).
🧂
Desalination (Small Scale)
Gas-fired Multi-Effect Distillation (MED) units for on-farm brackish water desalination. Eliminates tanker water dependency for remote farms — SAR 0.80/m³ vs SAR 4.50/m³ by tanker.
❄️
Cold Chain — Absorption Chillers
Gas absorption refrigeration for cold storage of produce, dairy, and meat. No compressors, no refrigerant — lower maintenance, longer life, 40% cheaper than electric refrigeration at Saudi electricity prices.
🌰
Roasting & Baking
Gas rotary ovens, roasters for nuts, seeds, coffee. Uniform heat transfer, programmable temperature profiles, and superior product quality vs. electric elements — at lower cost per batch.

Vision 2030
Aligned with Saudi Arabia's Food Security Agenda
🎯
National Agricultural Development Programme
TAQA Arabia's agricultural gas supply directly supports NADP goals to expand domestic food production. Gas enables year-round greenhouse cultivation and livestock expansion — critical for the 60% domestic production target.
🌿
Saudi Green Initiative
Switching farms from diesel to gas reduces agricultural CO₂ emissions by up to 50%. TAQA Arabia's network supports the Saudi Green Initiative's agricultural sustainability targets and reduces the sector's carbon intensity per tonne of food produced.
💧
Water-Energy Nexus
Saudi Arabia's water strategy requires maximising output per m³. Gas-powered pumping and gas-fired desalination improve water productivity — more food per drop of water, at lower energy cost. Supports NWC and Ministry of Environment targets.

MEWA Partnership
TAQA Arabia works directly with the Ministry of Environment, Water and Agriculture (MEWA) to identify priority agricultural zones for gas network expansion. Government co-funding available for qualifying agricultural hubs under Vision 2030 programmes.
