🚫
Running Out Mid-Cooking
Cylinder runs empty without warning — in the middle of a family meal, guests visiting, or Ramadan gatherings. No advance notice, no backup.
⏳
Waiting for Deliveries
Calling the delivery company, waiting hours or days, coordination issues. Delivery schedules don't fit your lifestyle — you wait on their terms.
💰
Unpredictable Costs
LPG prices fluctuate with global oil markets. You never know what the next cylinder will cost — impossible to budget household energy expenses reliably.
⚠️
Safety Concerns
Cylinder storage, aging hoses, regulator leaks. LPG is heavier than air — leaks accumulate at floor level creating explosion risk in kitchens and utility rooms.
🏠
Storage Space Wasted
Bulky cylinders take up kitchen, balcony, or utility room space. Many families keep spare cylinders "just in case" — taking up precious home space.
🌍
Environmental Footprint
Cylinder transport vehicles, packaging, and cylinder manufacturing all add to your carbon footprint — far higher than efficient piped gas distribution.

Safety First
Your Family's Safety — Our Priority
🔒
Odorant Added
Natural gas is naturally odourless — TAQA Arabia adds THT odorant (rotten egg smell) to every cubic meter. You'll smell any leak immediately, long before it reaches dangerous levels.
⚡
Automatic Shutoff Valves
Emergency shutoff installed at the meter and at each appliance. In case of a fire, seismic event, or detected leak, the system shuts automatically — no action needed.
🛡️
Certified Installation Only
All pipework, appliances, and connections are installed exclusively by TAQA Arabia's licensed technicians — no DIY permitted. Full civil defence compliance documentation provided.
📱
Smart Leak Detectors
Optional TAQA SmartHome gas detectors connect to your phone. Real-time alerts if any gas is detected — with automatic valve closure triggered remotely.
🔧
Annual Safety Check
Free annual inspection of all gas appliances, pipework, and meters by certified TAQA engineers — mandatory for continued supply and included in your tariff.
🚒
24/7 Emergency Response
Gas emergency hotline answered 24/7. Response target: on-site within 2 hours anywhere in our service area. No call centre — direct to a technician.
