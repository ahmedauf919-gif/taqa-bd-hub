The Problem
Saudi Fleet Operators Face a Fuel Crisis
💸
Diesel Costs Erode Margins
Fuel accounts for 30–40% of fleet operating costs. A 10% diesel price rise directly reduces a logistics company's EBITDA by 3–4 percentage points — with no easy offset.
💨
Emission Regulations Tightening
Saudi Arabia's General Authority of Meteorology (GAMET) and municipalities are introducing emission standards for commercial vehicles — especially in urban and port zones. Non-compliant fleets face fines and operating restrictions.
🏙️
Urban Air Quality Mandates
Riyadh, Jeddah, and Dammam are implementing "Green City" zones aligned with Vision 2030. Diesel heavy vehicles will face zone entry restrictions — CNG or electric is the path to compliance.
📊
ESG & Procurement Pressure
Government tenders, Aramco contracts, and international logistics clients increasingly mandate ESG compliance in supply chains. A diesel-only fleet is a procurement liability — CNG fleets win more contracts.

The Solution
Why CNG? The Economics Are Compelling
⛽
35% Lower Fuel Cost
CNG at SAR 1.08/SCF-equivalent vs diesel at SAR 1.67/litre. A truck doing 100,000 km/year on diesel pays ~SAR 83K in fuel. On CNG, that drops to ~SAR 54K — saving SAR 29K per truck annually.
🔧
Lower Maintenance Cost
CNG burns cleaner than diesel — no soot, no DPF regeneration, fewer oil changes. Engine life extends 20–30%. Brake pad life improves with regenerative braking on CNG buses. Total lifecycle cost 18% lower than diesel.
🌿
25% Less CO₂
CNG emits 15–25% less CO₂ per km than diesel. Near-zero particulate matter (PM2.5) and 90% less NOₓ. Supports your corporate sustainability report, ESG rating, and government contract eligibility.
🛡️
Safer Than Diesel
CNG is lighter than air — disperses upward in a leak. Diesel pools on the ground creating fire risk. CNG tanks are built to withstand impacts 4× higher than diesel tanks. Ignition temperature 100°C higher than petrol.
🔇
Quieter Operation
CNG engines are 50% quieter than diesel — significant for urban waste collection, airport operations, hospital logistics. Enables night operations in noise-restricted zones, improving fleet utilisation.
📜
Future-Proof Fuel
CNG infrastructure is bio-methane and hydrogen-blend ready. Your CNG station investment today can refuel renewable natural gas (RNG) and hydrogen blends tomorrow — protecting your CapEx against fuel transition.
