The Challenge

Saudi Energy Users Face Rising Cost & Reliability Pressure

💸 Cost Pressure

Electricity tariffs have risen sharply; diesel and LPG remain volatile, exposing consumers to unpredictable input costs.

🌍 Sustainability Mandates

Vision 2030 targets require lower carbon intensity across industry, transport and buildings.

⚠️ Supply Reliability

Cylinder-based LPG and diesel logistics create delivery delays, storage limits and operational risk.

📉 Competitive Disadvantage

Operators without piped gas access run at a structural cost disadvantage versus grid-connected peers.
